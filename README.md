##  Overview  
*My Hero Academia: Final Season* (Season 8) marks the epic conclusion to the long-running anime.  
It premiered on **October 4, 2025** in Japan.  
The season adapts the final arcs of the original manga by **Kōhei Horikoshi** — namely the *Final War Arc* and the *Epilogue Arc*.  
At its core: a climactic showdown between heroes and villains, as hopes, legacies, and the very future of hero society hang in the balance.  

---

##  Main Characters & Factions  
- **Izuku Midoriya (“Deku”)** – the young hero determined to become the greatest, even when facing impossible odds.  
- **Katsuki Bakugo** – explosive and prideful, but fiercely loyal.  
- **Shoto Todoroki** – calm, powerful, and driven by family redemption.  
- **All Might** – the Symbol of Peace, now a mentor fighting with wisdom and courage.  
- **Tomura Shigaraki & All For One** – the ultimate villains aiming to end the hero era.
